### 🔬 About Me

I am a **Geospatial Data Scientist and Computational Researcher** at **NCBC NEDUET** (National Center of Big Data & Cloud Computing), working at the intersection of:
* 🛰️ **Geospatial AI & Remote Sensing**: Developing scalable Earth Observation and hydrological data pipelines for climate-resilient water systems.
* 🧠 **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**: Designing self-supervised representation learning models for complex spatial-temporal hydrological networks.
* 🏙️ **Complex Systems & Simulation**: Crafting deterministic cellular automata and emergent multi-agent simulation architectures.
