### 🔬 About Me

I am a **Geospatial Data Scientist and Computational Researcher** at **NCBC NEDUET** (National Center of Big Data & Cloud Computing), working at the intersection of:
* 🛰️ **Physics-Informed Geospatial AI**: Integrating physical conservation laws with deep learning for climate and hydrological modeling.
* 🧠 **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**: Developing self-supervised representation learning frameworks for spatial-temporal environmental networks.
* 🏙️ **Complex Systems & Simulation**: Designing cellular automata architectures and autonomous multi-agent simulation engines.
